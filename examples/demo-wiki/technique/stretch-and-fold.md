# Stretch and fold

**Summary**: Gentle folds during bulk fermentation build strength in wet dough.

Folding trades effort for time. It is the usual way to develop [[gluten]] in a [[sourdough-loaf]] or [[ciabatta]], usually after an [[autolyse]].

## Frequency

Three or four sets during the first half of [[fermentation]] are typical.
